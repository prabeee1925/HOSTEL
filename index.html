How does this work? (read this first)
  • Step 1: Each student registers with their own name and shared room number. Up to 10 students can register in one room.
  • Step 2: Set the time you leave your room every day.
  • Step 3: You get an alarm 5 minutes before that time and again at that time. It repeats every 2 minutes (up to 5 times) until you confirm.
  • Step 4: Switch OFF the light and fan, choose OFF for both, and press I'm leaving the room.
  • The admin (warden) sees each room as: All OFF (done), Waiting (not time yet), Still ON (you said something is ON) or Not confirmed (time is over and nothing was marked).
  • Already switched OFF but forgot to mark it? Press the green button “I already switched both OFF”. It is on your status box and on the alarm message.
  • The app cannot sense the real switches. It trusts what you mark, so the warden can also check a room and press Mark OFF.
  • Alarms from the app ring while it is open. For alerts when your phone is locked, use the calendar alarm in Step 3. Rooms are saved online, so the warden sees every student.
